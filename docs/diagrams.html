2 · Field capture → geo-verification → AI (the closed loop)

sequenceDiagram
  participant O as Official (PWA)
  participant Q as Offline queue
  participant A as API :5000
  participant G as geoService
  participant E as AI engine :5001
  participant D as Dashboard
  O->>O: capture photo + GPS + checklist
  O->>Q: queue (offline-safe)
  Q->>A: replay multipart + geo-tag
  A->>G: haversine(site, device)
  G-->>A: verified | mismatch | suspicious
  A->>A: hash evidence + audit + socket emit
  A->>E: anomaly / risk / attendance
  E-->>A: anomalies + risk (never fails)
  A-->>D: risk + verdict + alert
